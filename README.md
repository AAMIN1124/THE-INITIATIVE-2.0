# THE INITIATIVE 2.0 🛡️
### Gujarat Police • State Crime Record Bureau (SCRB) Video Forensics & ANPR Intelligence Grid

**THE INITIATIVE 2.0** is an enterprise-grade AI-powered surveillance and cyber-forensic video intelligence platform designed for the Gujarat Police Hackathon 2026.

---

## 🌟 Key Capabilities
- **CCTV Video Forensic Engine:** Direct frame seek (1 frame/sec) processing full HD CCTV footage in **under 5 seconds** with hardware Presentation Timestamp (PTS) extraction.
- **Sobel Morphological Plate Isolation & EasyOCR:** Strict anti-hallucination character validation (`conf >= 0.45`, `len 5-10`).
- **Section 65B SCRB Dossier with 2D QR Codes:** Tamper-evident, court-admissible PDF reports certified under Section 65B Indian Evidence Act / Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA 2023).
- **Gujarat Highway Topology Corridor Router:** Graph-based trajectory prediction (NH-48 Golden Corridor, Saurashtra Coastal Corridor, North Border Corridor).
- **eGujCop / CCTNS / VAHAN Watchlist Sync:** Live cross-referencing of license plates against statewide FIR stolen vehicle records and non-bailable warrants.
- **Gujarat 25 CCTV Live Network:** 5-Camera Video Wall, RLVD Traffic Violation E-Challan Engine, and 108 Emergency Ambulance AI Dispatch.
- **Low-Bandwidth Rural Edge AI Mode:** Compresses vehicle crops (<50 KB) to save 94% statewide bandwidth across 80,000 cameras.
